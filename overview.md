Today we visited the Billings Estate. It is significantly smaller (less land) than the images from our readings. 
We played around with Polycam and lasers, measuring pieces of tomb stones. I have experience with Polycam but I do not with the laser we used in class. I prefer using Polycam over the laser because of the user-friendliness of the application and I enjoy the fact that it is as accessible as downloading an app and not purchasing an expensive laser device. 
The best trick when using the Polycam application is to move slowly to allow for several photos to be taken - the more the merrier in this case really pays off. When walking in the cemetery, I took two different Polycams of a tombstone, (uploaded in this repository- hopefully both are viewable) and one of them was significantly more distorted than the other because I was moving faster and I stumbled once. The second go was much better and the image created was significantly less distorted. 
Having access to these devices allows for more ability to record archaeological finds. I could never do an artist interpretation/rendering of the tombstones with the speed and accuracy that the polycam or lasers provide.
